About Me

Bridging Business Context & Data Engineering

"Data becomes truly powerful when rigorous engineering meets clear, human storytelling."

With an advanced academic grounding in Big Data, the Internet of Things, and Business Intelligence, I focus on building reliable bridges between complex raw telemetries and clear, actionable executive strategy.

My background centers around end-to-end data pipelines: transforming unstructured data, architecting high-performance SQL models, engineering ETL workflows, and authoring intuitive dashboards in Power BI. Whether standardizing corporate metrics or streamlining data ingestion pipelines, my aim is always pragmatic: delivering clarity, reducing manual overhead, and driving data-informed decisions.
